Mark S. Gorski is an Oregon criminal defense, DUII and traffic attorney, and the founder of Gorski Law. The Oregon State Bar's public directory lists him as an active member, Bar No. 912881, admitted on September 27, 1991. He founded his criminal defense practice in 1991 and represents clients throughout Oregon from a Portland-metro base.

## Who is Mark S. Gorski?

Mark is a trial lawyer. Peers know him as "Moose", which is also the name behind the firm's Facebook page, [trialmoose](https://www.facebook.com/trialmoose/). His full name is Mark Steven Gorski.

His practice is built around three kinds of cases that often overlap: [DUII defense](/duii-defense/), [traffic tickets and license protection](/traffic-tickets/), and [criminal defense](/criminal-defense/) from misdemeanors to felonies. In the firm's own description, Mark has defended thousands of Oregon drivers since 1991.

Traffic work has always been a big part of the practice. People who drive for a living, such as taxi and school bus drivers, truck drivers, self-employed landscapers and construction workers, need a clean driving record, and for them a single ticket can mean losing work. Everyday drivers often call because a conviction on their record may push up their insurance.
