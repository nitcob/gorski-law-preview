## What this website collects

- **Nothing through forms in this preview.** The contact form is disabled; it cannot send or store anything.
- **No tracking cookies and no analytics.** The site loads no third-party scripts, fonts or trackers.
- **Browser storage.** If you close the "concept preview" notice, your browser remembers that for the current tab (session storage). Nothing is sent anywhere.
- **Hosting logs.** Like any website, the hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and the page requested) for security and operations.
