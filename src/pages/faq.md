faq:
  - q: "What is the difference between DUI and DUII?"
    a: "Nothing practical. DUII is Oregon's legal term for what most states call DUI: driving under the influence of intoxicants. Under [ORS 813.010](https://oregon.public.law/statutes/ors_813.010) it covers driving with a blood alcohol content of 0.08% or more, or while under the influence of an intoxicant or a combination of intoxicants. See [DUI vs. DUII in Oregon](/blog/dui-vs-duii-oregon/)."
  - q: "How long do I have to request a DMV hearing after a DUII arrest?"
    a: "10 days from the date of arrest. If you failed a blood test, the 10 days run from the date DMV sends the notice of suspension ([ORS 813.410](https://oregon.public.law/statutes/ors_813.410)). See [the implied consent hearing](/duii-defense/dmv-implied-consent-hearing/)."
  - q: "Am I eligible for DUII diversion?"
    a: "Eligibility is set by [ORS 813.215](https://oregon.public.law/statutes/ors_813.215). Among other conditions: no DUII conviction and no diversion in the 15 years before the offense, no commercial driving privileges on the date of the offense, not driving a commercial motor vehicle, and no accident that caused a death or physical injury to someone else. The petition generally must be filed within 30 days of your first court appearance ([ORS 813.210](https://oregon.public.law/statutes/ors_813.210)). See [Oregon DUII diversion](/duii-defense/diversion/)."
  - q: "Is DUII a felony in Oregon?"
    a: "Usually not: DUII is a Class A misdemeanor. It is a Class C felony if the person has been convicted of DUII at least twice in the 10 years before the new offense, and once someone has been sentenced for felony DUII, any later DUII is a felony no matter how much time has passed ([ORS 813.011](https://oregon.public.law/statutes/ors_813.011)). [ORS 813.010](https://oregon.public.law/statutes/ors_813.010) has a separate felony rule for three prior convictions or juvenile adjudications in 10 years. See [repeat and felony DUII](/duii-defense/repeat-and-felony-duii/)."
  - q: "What is the minimum fine for a first DUII conviction?"
    a: "At least $1,000 for a first conviction in a vehicle other than a bicycle, and at least $2,000 if the blood alcohol content was 0.15% or more ([ORS 813.010](https://oregon.public.law/statutes/ors_813.010)). Other penalties and costs apply as well."
  - q: "Can a CDL holder get DUII diversion?"
    a: "No. A person who held commercial driving privileges on the date of the offense is not eligible ([ORS 813.215](https://oregon.public.law/statutes/ors_813.215)). See [CDL and commercial drivers](/traffic-tickets/cdl-commercial-drivers/)."
  - q: "Is a traffic ticket a crime in Oregon?"
    a: "A traffic violation is not a crime. Violations are punished by fines, not jail, and a violation conviction carries no disability based on conviction of a crime ([ORS 153.008](https://oregon.public.law/statutes/ors_153.008)). Some driving offenses are crimes, though: reckless driving, for example, is a Class A misdemeanor ([ORS 811.140](https://oregon.public.law/statutes/ors_811.140))."
