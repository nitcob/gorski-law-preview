Quick answers

Three things people ask first

  • DUI vs. DUIIDUII is Oregon's legal term for DUI

    Driving under the influence of intoxicants: a blood alcohol content of 0.08% or more, or driving while under the influence of an intoxicant. It is usually a Class A misdemeanor (ORS 813.010).

  • The DMV hearingAn implied consent hearing is about your license

    It decides whether the DMV suspension after a DUII arrest is valid. It is separate from the criminal case, and the request window is short (ORS 813.410).

  • TicketsA traffic violation is not a crime in Oregon

    Violations carry fines, not jail, and are not criminal convictions (ORS 153.008). Some driving offenses are crimes, though: reckless driving is a Class A misdemeanor (ORS 811.140).

Drive for a living?

For some drivers, one ticket is a job problem

Taxi and school bus drivers, truck drivers, self-employed landscapers and construction workers, anyone who needs a CDL: they all need a clean driving record. Everyday drivers worry about insurance going up after a conviction lands on their record.

Commercial drivers face extra rules. Federal regulations can disqualify a CDL holder after certain serious traffic violations, and Oregon DUII diversion is not available to anyone who held commercial driving privileges on the date of the offense (ORS 813.215).
