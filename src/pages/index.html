The three practice areas overlap more than people expect. A DUII arrest is a criminal case and a license case at the same time, and for a commercial driver one ticket can turn into a job problem.

Drive for a living?

For some drivers, one ticket is a job problem

Truck, taxi and school bus drivers, self-employed landscapers and construction crews: anyone who needs a CDL or a clean record to keep working. Federal rules can disqualify a CDL holder after certain serious traffic violations, and Oregon DUII diversion is not open to anyone who held commercial driving privileges on the date of the offense (ORS 813.215).

Is a DUII the same as a DUI?

Yes. DUII, driving under the influence of intoxicants, is Oregon's legal name for what most states call DUI. A first offense is usually a Class A misdemeanor (ORS 813.010). More in DUI vs. DUII in Oregon.

I was just arrested for DUII. What is the first deadline?

Usually the DMV hearing request: 10 days from the date of arrest, or 10 days from DMV's notice if you failed a blood test (ORS 813.410). Read what to do in the first 10 days.

Is a traffic ticket a crime in Oregon?

Most are not. A traffic violation carries a fine, not jail, and is not a criminal conviction (ORS 153.008). Some driving offenses are crimes, though: reckless driving is a Class A misdemeanor (ORS 811.140).
